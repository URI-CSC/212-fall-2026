Reads the growth.csv that bench writes (columns increment, n, copies, grows, ms). One line per increment c. The dashed lines are the prediction from lecture, n^2 / (2c) - n / 2 copies, drawn only for the copies plot. On log-log axes a straight line of slope 1 is linear growth and slope 2 is quadratic: read the slopes, not the heights. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file is read by this page, in your browser.

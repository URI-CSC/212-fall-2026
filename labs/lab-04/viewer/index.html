Reads the log that ./session script.txt > session.log writes. Each step is one command from the script, followed by what your print() wrote. Pixels changed by the step have a white outline. A card is one paint: (row, col), the color before, and the color after. The top of each stack is drawn first, with a blue outline; only the top cards of a stack are drawn. Keys: ← → step, space play. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file is read by this page, in your browser.

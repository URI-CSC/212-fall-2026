Reads the log that ./session small.txt 6 > session.log writes. Each step is one push, followed by what your print() wrote and the max your class returned. The shaded band is the window: the last k values. The dashed line joins the values in the deque, in order. Values a step removes are outlined: red when popped from the back, orange when popped from the front. Keys: ← → step, space play. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file is read by this page, in your browser.
